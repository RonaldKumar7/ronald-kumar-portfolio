Structured decision-making for executive recommendations backed by data.

Performance Management Framework

Core Leadership Principles

  • Data-First Decision Making: The leader should not defend a decision simply because they made it. If the data shows that an ICP, process, tool or strategy is not working, the right response is to own the decision, understand why it failed, preserve what is working and change course.
  • GTM Accountability: Revenue should not be viewed as the sole responsibility of Business Development. Marketing, Business Development and Account Executives should operate toward a common commercial outcome, with clear ownership at each stage: demand creation, qualified conversations, opportunity acceptance, sales execution and conversion.

Leadership Case Studies

Building a BD Function from Zero to One

  • Start with baseline data and current-state assessment
  • Define ICP and account-prioritization logic
  • Create messaging and outbound playbooks
  • Build qualification and SDR-AE handoff rules
  • Recruit, onboard and coach the team
  • Establish KPI, pipeline and review rhythms
  • Test → measure → improve → scale

Creating Cross-Functional GTM Accountability

  • Marketing owns demand creation and lead quality
  • SDR owns qualified conversations and accurate opportunity creation
  • AE owns opportunity progression and conversion
  • Leadership reviews the complete funnel rather than blaming one function
  • Disagreements are resolved through specific account and opportunity data

Building an Independent Team

  • Set transparent expectations
  • Give regular feedback
  • Coach based on individual potential
  • Document processes and playbooks
  • Develop ownership rather than dependence
  • Build a leadership bench so the function can perform when the leader is absent
